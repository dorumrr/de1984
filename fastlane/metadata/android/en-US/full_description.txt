De1984 is a Firewall and Package Manager for Android that works with or without root.

The name <b>De1984</b> comes from George Orwell's Nineteen Eighty-Four. It stands for resisting surveillance and taking back control of your device and data.

<b>FIREWALL FEATURES</b>

- Four firewall methods: iptables (root, or Shizuku in root mode), ConnectivityManager (Shizuku, Android 13+), NetworkPolicyManager (Shizuku, chosen manually) and VPN (no root, works everywhere). Picked automatically for your device. iptables blocks at kernel level
- On ROMs without POLICY_REJECT_ALL, NetworkPolicyManager only blocks metered background data, not WiFi
- Block WiFi, Mobile Data and Roaming separately with iptables and VPN (Roaming is part of Mobile Data). ConnectivityManager and NetworkPolicyManager block an app on all networks or none
- Block LAN access, enforced only by iptables
- "Block All by Default" (allowlist) or "Allow All by Default" (blocklist). Switching resets every app's WiFi, Mobile, Roaming and LAN rules to the new default, with no confirmation. System-recommended apps keep theirs, and critical and VPN apps keep theirs only while "Allow Firewall Critical Packages" is on
- Captive portal settings (root or Shizuku). They stay changed after De1984 is uninstalled
- Screen-off blocking to save battery and data
- Rules follow network changes in real time

<b>PACKAGE MANAGEMENT (Shizuku or root)</b>

- Enable or disable system apps, force stop apps, uninstall system and user apps
- Filter by system/user and enabled/disabled, and search

<b>PRIVACY FIRST</b>

- No tracking, analytics or telemetry
- Local-only data storage
- No proprietary libraries, no Google Play Services
- 100% open source (MIT License), buildable from source

<b>REQUIREMENTS</b>

- Android 8.0 (API 26) or higher

<b>PERMISSIONS</b>

- ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: apply rules per network
- BIND_VPN_SERVICE: the VPN firewall
- QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES: list installed apps
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS (optional): new app installs
- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: start the firewall after a reboot, if it was on
- FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: keep the firewall running
- REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS (optional): stop Android killing the firewall
- KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES: last fallback for force stop
- Shizuku API: talk to Shizuku or Sui
- INTERACT_ACROSS_USERS: read apps in other profiles. When one exists, De1984 grants it to itself through root or Shizuku, without asking
- WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS, CHANGE_COMPONENT_ENABLED_STATE: only checked, to show status
- REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES, INSTALL_SHORTCUT, ACCESS_SUPERUSER: declared, not used
- Root or Shizuku (optional): the iptables, ConnectivityManager and NetworkPolicyManager firewalls, package management, boot protection and captive portal settings
